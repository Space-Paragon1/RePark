RePark design system. One set of tokens, type, icons and components for the website, iPhone and Android.

Blue means RePark is handling it. Yellow means a person needs to act. Every status pairs an icon, a word and a colour. Sections: 01 Logo · 02 Colour · 03 Type · 04 Space, shape and motion · 05 Icons · 06 Components in light and dark.
