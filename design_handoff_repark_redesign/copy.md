Voice: calm, neighbourly, plain, firm about privacy. Sentence case, no exclamation marks, no emoji.

## Website home
- Kicker: ANONYMOUS PARKING ALERTS
- H1: Blocked in? Tell the owner in under a minute. Neither of you learns who the other is.
- Plate label: Enter the plate that's blocking you · Button: Continue · Hint: Next you sign in with your phone number. We keep the plate.
- Facts: Push, or a text — Owners without the app get a text message. / Reply in two taps — Moving now, Already moved or Incorrect report. / Closes in 24 hours — Every report ends with a clear outcome.
- How it works: Four moments, each one visible to you. / You enter the plate — Type it, confirm where you are and pick the problem. / We alert the owner — By push, or by text if they don't use the app. You see which, and when. / The owner replies — Moving now, Already moved or Incorrect report. / The report closes — If there's no reply, it closes as No response. If the plate isn't registered, the owner has 2 hours to join.
- Privacy: RePark stands in the middle. Names, numbers and exact locations stay with us. Here is exactly what each side gets. / Never: who reported it, their number, their note or where they were. / Never: the owner's name or number.
- Owners: Register your plates so people can reach you without a note on the windscreen. When someone's blocked in by your car, you get a push, or a text if you don't have the app. You reply in two taps and they see it straight away. They never learn who you are. Button: Register a plate.
- App band: Get RePark on your phone. Alerts arrive as notifications, with Moving now and Already moved right on the lock screen.
